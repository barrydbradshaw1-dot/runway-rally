Runway Rally Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-18. Effective [date]. Runway Rally is made by Nexus Data Engineering, Georgia, USA. Questions: support@nexusdataengineering.com.

1. Who this is for

Runway Rally is a math practice game for students in grades 3 to 8, many of them under 13. Schools and teachers set it up for their classes. When a school uses Runway Rally, we act as the school's service provider: the school decides how student information is used, and we use it only to run Runway Rally for that school. Each teacher confirms, when they make a class, that their school has approved Runway Rally.

2. What we collect

We collect as little as we can. We do not ask for a student's real name, email address, birthday, age, phone number, photo, address or location, and there is no chat.

  • To sign in: a class code from the teacher and a nickname the student makes up. Nicknames that look like a full real name, or that contain rude words, are turned down, and a teacher can rename a student at any time. If the school opens Runway Rally from Brightspace, we instead receive a coded Brightspace user ID and course ID, and we make up a nickname such as "Designer 4821". A school can choose to have us show first name and last initial instead.
  • Learning progress: which problems a student answered, right or wrong, hints used, skills check results, skill levels, time practiced each day, streaks, the look the student picked for their designer, and the rewards earned in the game.
  • Sign-in codes: a recovery code and a family progress link, so progress follows the student to a new device. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) form of these, so we cannot read them.
  • Technical information: the student's time zone offset (to count days correctly, not saved) and IP address (held for about a minute to stop abuse, not saved).

We do not use cookies, advertising, analytics services or tracking from other companies. The app stores a sign-in key in the browser so the student stays signed in.

3. How we use it

Only to run Runway Rally: to choose the next problem, show progress to the student, their teacher and their family, send a practice grade to the school's Brightspace gradebook when the school turns that on, keep the service secure, and fix and improve the game. We may use information with all identifiers removed to improve how the game teaches.

We never sell student information, never use it for advertising, and never build a profile of a student for any purpose other than their school learning.

4. Who can see it

  • The student.
  • The student's teacher, for their own class only.
  • Anyone holding the student's family progress link, which shows a read-only summary. The student can turn the old link off by making a new one.
  • The school's Brightspace, which receives the practice grade.
  • Our hosting provider, DigitalOcean, which runs our servers in [the United States: confirm region] and cannot use the data for itself.

We share information otherwise only if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or if Runway Rally is sold, in which case the new owner must keep this promise.

5. Children under 13 (COPPA)

When a school uses Runway Rally for learning, the school gives consent on behalf of parents for us to collect the information above, as federal guidance allows. We use it only for the school's educational purpose. Parents can ask the school to see the information, or to have it deleted, at any time, and can write to us directly.

6. Schools and FERPA

Student information that a school provides or that comes from a student's use of Runway Rally for school is part of the school's education records. The school owns and controls it. We act as a "school official" under the school's direct control, use the records only for the purpose the school set, and don't disclose them except as FERPA allows. A teacher can download everything we keep about one student from the teacher console, to answer a parent's request to see it.

7. Georgia law

We follow Georgia's Student Data Privacy, Accessibility, and Transparency Act. We don't use student data for targeted advertising, don't sell it, and don't build profiles except for school purposes. When a school or district asks us to delete a student's information, we delete it within 45 days.

8. How we keep it safe

All connections use HTTPS. Sign-in codes and keys are stored only in scrambled form. Answers are checked on our server. Each teacher has their own login and sees only their own classes, and every deletion, rename and download of a student's data is logged. Only one administrator (the developer) can reach the server, behind a firewall, with SSH keys. Database backups are encrypted. Server logs are rotated so they don't pile up.

If student information is ever accessed by someone who shouldn't have it, we will tell affected schools within [72 hours] of learning of it, and help them notify families.

9. How long we keep it

We keep a student's information while their school uses Runway Rally, and never longer than this schedule:

  • Answer history: each answer and finished problem is deleted after 12 months. The problem a student is working on is kept.
  • Students who stop playing: a student who hasn't played for 2 years is deleted, with all their information.
  • When a class ends: a class with a last day set is deleted, with all its students, 30 days after that day. When a school's agreement ends, we set that day or delete the school's classes, so its information is gone within 30 days.
  • Deletion requests: a teacher, or we, can delete a student or a whole class at once from the teacher console, well within Georgia's 45 days. A student can also delete their own data from the app.
  • Backups: the last 14 backups are kept, so deleted information leaves the backups within about 14 days.

10. Your choices

  • Parents and students can ask their school, or write to support@nexusdataengineering.com, to see a student's information, correct it, or delete it. We will verify the request with the school before acting.
  • Schools can download their class data, or one student's full record, from the teacher console at any time, and can delete any student or a whole class there themselves or ask us to.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects how we use student information, we will tell schools before the change takes effect and won't apply it to information already collected without the school's agreement.
