# Runway Rally Privacy Policy (DRAFT for lawyer review)

Operator: Nexus Data Engineering, Georgia, USA. Contact: support@nexusdataengineering.com
Last updated: [date]

This draft describes what the software does today. A lawyer must review it before any school uses it.

## Who this is for
Runway Rally is a math game for middle school students. Schools or teachers choose to use it. When a school
uses it, the school authorizes our collection of student information for educational use only, and we act on the school's behalf.

## What we collect
- **Class code and nickname.** Students pick a nickname. We ask students not to use their real name. Teachers can see every nickname.
- **Game progress.** Skill levels, answers (right or wrong, hints used), cards, items and "Thread" earned, commissions completed, and
  simple activity records (for example, "took a hint"). We use these to adjust difficulty and show the teacher where help is needed.
- **If the class launches from a learning system (LTI, for example Brightspace):** an anonymous identifier from the school's system,
  the course identifier, and the link needed to send grades back. We do not store real names or email addresses unless the school
  turns that setting on. If it does, we store only first name and last initial.

## What we do not collect
- No photos, voice, video, precise location, birth dates, email addresses, phone numbers, or government IDs.
- **Avatar looks.** A student's chosen skin tone, hair and clothing are kept only on that student's own device. They are never sent to us.
  We do not record or label race or ethnicity.
- **Selfie color match.** If a student chooses a selfie to suggest skin and hair colors, the photo is read on the device,
  immediately discarded, and never uploaded, saved or shown.
- No advertising, no ad trackers, no third-party analytics, and no sale of student information.

## How we use it
Only to run the game, adapt questions, report progress to the student's teacher, and send grades to the school's learning system when the school asks.
We do not use student information to train AI models, build profiles for any other purpose, or market to students.

## Who sees it
The student, the student's teacher or school administrators, and our hosting provider (DigitalOcean, which stores data on our server).
We do not share student information with anyone else, except where the law requires.

## Security
Encrypted connections (HTTPS); sign-in tokens stored only as hashes; teacher access protected by a secret or by the school's LTI launch;
rate limiting; a strict content security policy. Backups: [describe encryption and retention]. If we learn of a breach affecting student information,
we notify the school without unreasonable delay [state timeline per agreement].

## Keeping and deleting data
We keep student data while the school uses the service. On request from the school, a parent, or a student's teacher, we delete a student or
a whole class (see Operator Runbook). We also delete data for students inactive for [12] months. At the end of a school agreement we delete or return the data.

## Parents and students
Parents can ask the school or us to see, correct or delete their child's information. Write to the contact above.

## Changes
We will tell schools before making material changes.
